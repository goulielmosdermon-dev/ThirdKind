Domogroup featured in NouPou

Eight decades of working with wood, and the next chapter for the Greek company.

From 1947 to the next chapter

The NouPou feature follows the journey from Efthymis Chalkidis' woodworking shop in Katerini to today's Domogroup. The company marks its eightieth anniversary in 2027.

Spaces for meeting and creating

The renewed showrooms let visitors discover the materials and discuss complete proposals. In Vari, a 1,100 sq m space brings Living and Contract applications together.

Two directions, shared support

Living covers kitchens, wardrobes and residential furniture. Contract focuses on windows, staircases, pergolas, doors and structural applications. Design and technical support accompany delivery.

A new production base

The move to Kouvaras strengthens production, storage and distribution, supporting development in Greece and abroad.

Working with the architectural team

Engineers and architects participate from the start, analysing drawings and technical solutions. The two Kolonaki showrooms support everyday collaboration.

People, character, longevity

The team, individual solutions and sustainability remain central values. The vision connects technical precision, creativity and transparency with spaces that stand the test of time.
