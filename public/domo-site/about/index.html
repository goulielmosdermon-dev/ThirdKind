ABOUT

Wood is
our starting point.

A Greek company turning the possibilities of wood into spaces with individual character.

Our experience begins in 1947. Today, knowledge of wood processing meets contemporary design and construction flexibility.

We work with designers, architects, scientific institutions, construction companies and private clients. For residences, hotels and professional spaces, we combine wood, marble, stone, glass and metal with equal attention to function and aesthetics.
