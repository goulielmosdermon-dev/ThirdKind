Our Products Woodwork for every part of your space.

From kitchens and wardrobes to doors, stairs and custom furniture, we shape wood around the needs of your home or business. Our Living and Contract teams combine traditional craft with contemporary materials to create practical, individual solutions for interiors and outdoor spaces.

Explore our products ↗
Production Made in Greece. From the first cut to the final detail.

Our products are manufactured in Greece, bringing experienced craftspeople and modern equipment together under one roof. With wood expertise rooted in 1947 and production facilities in Kouvaras, we work closely with designers, architects and clients to turn a design into a carefully finished construction.

Discover our production ↗
Everywhere From measurement to installation, wherever your project takes us.

We support each project through measuring, design, manufacture, transport and installation. Working with architects, hoteliers and private clients, our teams deliver wooden constructions for homes, hospitality and professional spaces throughout Greece and abroad.

Explore business solutions ↗
Innovation New possibilities for a material we know by heart.

We develop bespoke wooden solutions in collaboration with designers, architects, engineers and Greek scientific institutions. Combining wood with materials such as metal, glass, stone and marble lets us create functional constructions with a distinct identity, from wall and ceiling systems to custom structural details.
