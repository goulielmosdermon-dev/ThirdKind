Our knowledge of wood.
Your next idea.

Since 1947, we have developed the craft of working with wood, shaping contemporary spaces and distinctive constructions.

Domogroup brings industrial production together with technical knowledge and the flexibility of a creative partner. We design and work with solid timber alongside designers, architects, construction companies and private clients in Greece and abroad.

Our construction experience forms the foundation for solutions that meet today's needs: practical, accessible and individual. Alongside wood, we work with marble, stone, glass and metal to serve the design as a whole.

Our production and distribution facilities are based in Kouvaras. At our Vari–Koropi showroom and our Living and Contract spaces in Kolonaki, our team explores your project's needs and the possibilities for bringing it to life.
