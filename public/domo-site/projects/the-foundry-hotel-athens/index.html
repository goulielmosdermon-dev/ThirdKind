A wooden identity.

Category
Hotels / Contract
Scope
Woodworking construction

In Athens' historic Psyrri neighbourhood, The Foundry Hotel Athens combines an industrial aesthetic with a distinctive vintage approach.

Domogroup helped shape the boutique hotel's identity, translating the architectural drawings into construction with close attention to every detail.

Traditional and contemporary woodworking materials come together so that each construction complements the building's character and the way its spaces function. The design is by Miltos Portokalis.
