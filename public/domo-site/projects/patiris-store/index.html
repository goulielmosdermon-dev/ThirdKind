Scope
Woodworking & showroom fit-out

Woodworking for the Patiris showroom in Palaio Faliro. A restrained, warm environment that allows the products to take centre stage.

Domogroup undertook the fit-out of the store and showroom to bring out the character and quality of its products. The approach combines clean lines, considered organisation and constructions designed specifically for the space.

Tile displays, furniture and sanitaryware presentation stands form a coherent visitor journey. Wooden cladding and concealed doors provide a calm backdrop, while integrated lighting brings out textures and details.

Function and movement through the showroom guide the composition, ensuring that every construction serves both the product and the visitor.
